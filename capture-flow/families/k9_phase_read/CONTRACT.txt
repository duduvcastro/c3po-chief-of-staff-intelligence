3. ORDER OF USE

  1  E0 (K4) creates the K9 tree and delivers the runner; K3-K9 writes the env files and the emitter password.
  2  TREE (K9R): its receipt must be KNOWN_COMPLETE (K9_TREE_AS_REQUIRED_ALL_OBSERVED). Its rows and boot are bound
     into every K9 request of the week. A reboot invalidates them: a new TREE read, new sheets.
  3  Each eve: readiness_probe (PROBE) -> the chain of NOTE 5.7, each *_result (RESULT) read at or after its
     launch's run_not_after; each morning: policy_read (POLICY), capture_result (RESULT).

4. WHAT A RECEIPT MEANS FOR THE CHAIN

  exit 0, KNOWN_COMPLETE      RESULT: phase_result COMPLETE: the step's receipt is valid, its container exited 0, its
                              outputs are on the host as the receipt says. PROBE: readiness READY. POLICY: the
                              installed files and the worker are as signed, now inside the policy window. TREE: the
                              tree as required.
  exit 2, MISMATCH outcome    RESULT: read phase_result: FAILED or UNCERTAIN ends the phase for D (ORD:33); COMPLETE
                              with a metadata finding is for the reviewer to judge (DESIGN.md 2.1). PROBE: readiness
                              NOT_READY -> readiness_recheck (NOTE 5.7 rule 2); any other finding -> UNKNOWN, treat as
                              not ready. POLICY, TREE: a finding holds the operations that depend on them.
  exit 2, PARTIAL             something could not be observed: RESULT phase_result UNCERTAIN; PROBE readiness UNKNOWN.
  exit 1, REFUSED             nothing was observed; nothing was touched.
  exit 3 / no receipt         UNCERTAIN. A PROBE may have left its container to the engine for at most 36 s.

  K9R writes no claim and removes no container. Single use of a READ request is the dispatcher's spawn.claim plus the
  SPARE rule (NOTE 4.1, 10.1).
