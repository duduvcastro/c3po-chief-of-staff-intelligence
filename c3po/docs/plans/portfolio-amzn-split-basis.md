# AMZN: explicit historical quantity basis

The owner confirmed that the historical AMZN acquisition quantities are already restated in post-20:1 units. Multiplying those quantities by pre-split raw closes inflates historical value and creates an artificial loss at the split boundary.

This change uses split-only normalization for AMZN before the 2022-06-06 trading session. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872422000009/amzn-20220309.htm (20:1; adjusted trading starts June 6). Raw closes are divided by 20 only when the account explicitly declares restated AMZN units. Provider adjusted_close is deliberately not used because it may incorporate dividends. The ledger, acquisition totals, fees, and current valuation remain unchanged.

The owner confirmed that only AMZN historical quantities are restated; Fable accepted this scope in #348 comment 5842083445. OWNER_CONFIRMED_RESTATED therefore defaults to AMZN in code. No host/.env mutation is needed. An explicitly empty C3PO_PORTFOLIO_SPLIT_ADJUSTED_SYMBOLS disables the declaration; an absent variable uses the confirmed default. Other symbols are not inferred. With an unknown basis, affected historical periods return N/D instead of a fabricated result. A ledger in original units with the explicit 20:1 event dated June 6 keeps raw historical closes. A conflicting explicit event plus restated declaration refuses the affected historical calculation. Mixed quantity bases require separate reconciliation; no automatic inference or ledger rewrite is allowed. This is narrowly scoped to the confirmed AMZN 2022 action, not a general corporate-actions engine.

Validation uses synthetic prices and quantities only: equivalent original/restated ledgers, June loss based on economically continuous prices, exact trading boundary, ambiguous and double-adjustment refusal, other symbols unchanged, and no persisted ledger changes. Current summary remains independently calculated; N/D historical results must not be presented as proof that current holdings are reconciled.
