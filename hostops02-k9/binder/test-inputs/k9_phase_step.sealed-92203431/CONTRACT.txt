3. WHAT A RECEIPT MEANS

  KNOWN_COMPLETE (exit 0): the step's container was created, proved again and started as built (LAUNCH: whether the
    step completed is the RESULT read of K9R), or ran and wrote a COMPLETE receipt (ATTACHED), or was removed (CLEANUP).
  KNOWN_REFUSAL (exit 1): nothing was created, no claim. The dispatcher's claim still spends the GO.
  KNOWN_PARTIAL (exit 2): the claim exists; the receipt says what else happened (removal-only, a step that ran and did
    not complete, an effect that failed or is uncertain). The attempt key is spent: nothing is repeated (ORD:33).
  UNCERTAIN (exit 3): state unknown; a K9R RESULT read and the owner decide.
